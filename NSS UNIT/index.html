About NSS

National Service Scheme (NSS)

The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a premier youth-led program under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India. Launched in 1969, its primary focus is to develop the personality and character of students through voluntary community service.

Guided by the operational motto, "Not Me But You," NSS underlines the belief that the welfare of an individual is ultimately dependent on the welfare of society as a whole.

Our Vision and Objectives

The NSS unit at our institution aims to bridge the gap between higher education and community needs. Through structured activities, our student volunteers learn to:

  • Understand the community in which they work and identify local challenges.
  • Develop a sense of social responsibility and civic readiness.
  • Acquire leadership qualities and a democratic attitude.
  • Practice national integration and social harmony.
